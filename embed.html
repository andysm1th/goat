Embed the goat

Put it on any page with these two lines:

<script type="module" src="%BASE_URL%text-goat.js"></script>
<text-goat></text-goat>

Left unsized, it is full width at 16:9. Give it a size, like the 70vh box at the top of this page, and it fills it: the goat stays centred and the lines and floor run on to the edges. It stops drawing while scrolled out of view or in a background tab.

It renders in a shadow root, so your page's styles cannot reach it. This page sets wide letter-spacing on everything and dashed hot-pink borders on every button, and neither goat shows a trace.

Options

Attributes on <text-goat>, all optional:

fit="contain"
Show the whole 16:9 frame, letterboxed. The default, fill, covers the box.
controls="pause" or "none"
A pause button only, or no buttons at all.
paused, techno, muted
Start paused, in Techno, or in Techno without sound. Also properties, alongside play() and pause().
text="…"
The word or phrase Techno runs along the lines.
label="…"
What screen readers announce in place of the animation.
frame-rate="30"
At most this many frames a second. The default is 60.
src="…"
Your own copy of the frames: the folder holding manifest.json. Without it they load from beside text-goat.js.

Colours are custom properties on the element: --goat-background, --goat-foreground, --goat-accent (the goat in Techno), --goat-techno-background and --goat-techno-text. Links or buttons inside it with slot="controls" join its control bar.

Example

The whole frame, a pause button only, and the page's own colours:

<text-goat fit="contain" controls="pause"
  style="--goat-background: #f5f5f5; --goat-foreground: #f73928">
</text-goat>

It reports changes as events: goat:ready, goat:play, goat:pause, goat:mode, goat:mute and goat:error. The goat at the top last said:
